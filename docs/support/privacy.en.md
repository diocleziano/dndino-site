# Privacy Policy — DnDino and DnDino Hero

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy covers both applications:

- **DnDino for macOS**, designed for Game Masters to manage adventures, characters, combats, places, maps and notes.
- **DnDino Hero for iPhone and iPad**, designed for players to create and manage characters, spells, equipment and notes. The guide and some technical references may also use its former name, **DnDino Companion**.

## Data used and purposes

The apps process content you enter or import, such as player and character names, character sheets, adventures, notes, drawings, images, equipment and custom content. Session features may also use messages, dice results and character state updates.

This data is used to provide the functions you request: content management, saving, backup, restoration, export and sharing during sessions.

No dedicated account with the developer is required. App content is not automatically sent to servers operated by the developer.

## Local storage

DnDino stores data primarily on the user's Mac; DnDino Hero stores it primarily on the user's iPhone or iPad. The apps may keep databases, images, preferences and local backups within their respective data containers. Exported files are saved to destinations chosen by the user.

## iCloud and backups

When iCloud is available to the app, content and images may be stored in the user's iCloud account for backup, synchronization or restoration. In DnDino Hero these operations may run automatically, including when the app enters the background or at a subsequent launch, according to the app's behavior and service availability.

You can manage the apps' iCloud access through Apple's settings. The developer does not receive your Apple Account credentials or have independent access to your iCloud storage. Apple provides this service under its [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Connecting DnDino and DnDino Hero

You can use Hero without connecting it to DnDino. If you enable a session connection, the apps use Apple's local networking technologies to discover and connect devices. They may exchange the device name, technical identifiers and session information for this purpose.

Depending on the features you use and the sharing permissions enabled, connected devices may exchange character sheets and updates, images, dice results and messages between player and Game Master. Updates may continue automatically while live synchronization is active. The connection uses the encryption provided by Apple's Multipeer Connectivity sessions, without a session server operated by the developer.

You can disconnect, disable live synchronization or revoke local network access in system settings. These actions stop subsequent exchanges but do not delete copies already received by other participants.

## Images, files and permissions

Images and files you select are used for the requested features and may be included in backups or content you choose to share. The apps do not request unrestricted access to your personal content. You can manage permissions in macOS, iOS or iPadOS settings.

## Advertising, support and external services

The apps do not use advertising tracking systems, profile users for advertising or sell personal data.

If you contact support, your message, email address and any attachments you send are used to handle your request and kept for as long as necessary for that purpose. Opening external links, including the online guide, accesses services governed by their respective privacy policies.

## Retention, deletion and control

Content remains on devices and in backups for as long as the user or sharing recipients retain it. You can delete content using the apps' controls; Hero also provides **Reset Application** to erase local data and return the app to its initial state.

Local deletion does not automatically remove all exported copies, iCloud backups or data already received on other devices. After resetting Hero, you can choose to recover an available backup or start with empty data; in the latter case, the next backup may replace the previous one. Use Apple's tools to manage or remove iCloud copies, and contact recipients about shared copies.

## Security and contact

The apps use security and permission mechanisms provided by Apple operating systems and technologies. Also protect the devices and accounts you use to manage your data.

For questions about privacy in **DnDino or DnDino Hero**, or data sent to support, contact [dndino.app@gmail.com](mailto:dndino.app@gmail.com).
