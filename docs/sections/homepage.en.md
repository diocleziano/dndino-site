# Home: GM Command Desk

In DnDino 2.0.0, Home is the **GM Command Desk**. The greeting uses your Dungeon Master name when set, and the layout adapts to the window without scrolling.

![GM Command Desk with four heroes and shortcuts to the latest location and combat](../images/homepage/command-desk-2-0.jpg){ .img-hero }

## Resume a campaign {#resume-adventure}

The left panel shows your last opened adventure or, if you haven’t opened one yet, the most recently updated adventure. Its cover and stats give you a quick view of completed quests, locations (excluding quests), sessions and finished combats.

**Open adventure** goes to the dashboard. **Go to the last visited place** and **Go to the latest combat** appear when the relevant data is available. [Adventure dashboard](adventure.md).

## Adventure Heroes {#adventure-heroes}

Linked heroes appear on the right. Up to three have side-by-side portraits; four use a 2 × 2 gallery; five or more use a compact grid. With more than eight heroes, a counter shows how many are not displayed. Heroes without an image show their initial.

Large portraits fade at the edges. Automatic framing tries to keep the face visible without changing the original file. Hover over a hero to see HP and AC, or click to open the read-only sheet with adventure-specific values.

## First adventure {#first-adventure}

When the library is empty, **Create your first adventure** opens the form directly. **Go to Adventures** opens the library, where you can also import a campaign. The illustrated welcome suggests adding characters, locations and quests.

![Illustrated welcome with the button to create a first adventure](../images/homepage/first-adventure-2-0.jpg){ .img-shot }

## Quick Access {#quick-access}

Open **Adventures**, **Heroes, NPCs and Monsters**, **Equipment**, **Rules**, **Name Generator** and **Settings** directly from Home.
