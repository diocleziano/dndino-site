# Official SRD Content and Duplicates

## A ready-to-use library

DnDino includes **SRD 5.2.1** offline: 330 monsters and animals, 339 spells, 17 feats and 416 equipment entries. Equipment comprises 38 weapons, 13 armor entries, 25 tools, 82 adventuring-gear entries and **258 magic items**.

Content follows the app language: English, Italian, French, German or Spanish. Both Portuguese variants use English. Changing language creates no copies and preserves character, place and combat references. Personal text is not translated automatically.

## Show, customize and restore

**Official content**, in **Settings → General** and the relevant lists, is enabled by default. Turning it off hides the catalog, not personal content or existing references. **Hide** removes one entry from the list without deleting it. Re-enabling the source restores hidden monsters, spells and equipment; feats also have a dedicated restore menu.

- Editing an official spell or feat creates a **personal copy**; the original stays available.
- Official monsters and equipment can be customized directly. Edited fields remain personal; untouched fields follow the selected language.
- **Restore official version** removes customizations while retaining identity, images and references. For monsters it also removes structured attacks and spell assignments added to the base sheet: review the confirmation carefully.

Find magic items under **Adventuring Gear → Magic Items**. Descriptions retain rarity, attunement requirements and variants; weights and prices are not invented when absent from the source.

## Manage duplicate characters

Open **Manage duplicates** from the character list. Matching recognizes equivalent names, including official translations; approximate similarity does not trigger an automatic merge.

All sheets are kept by default. Compare stats and images, choose the sheet to retain, then press **Apply** only when ready. Personal duplicates are merged into the chosen sheet while compatible references are preserved; official duplicates are hidden. Incompatible situations or active combats block the operation. The warning identifies the character, encounter and adventure and offers **Show place** when available.

You can also share only a cover image without merging sheets. Later changing or removing one sheet's image does not change the other's.

**Warning:** the chosen sheet keeps its own values, text, attacks and spells. Those from deleted personal sheets are not combined and are lost. Review the confirmation and make a backup first.

## Imports, exports and licenses

Exports do not needlessly replicate the entire built-in library. They retain personal content and customizations supported by that export type; adventures include the sheets they use. Imports cannot overwrite SRD originals. See [Import and Export](import-export.md). Source and attribution appear in **Settings → Licenses**.
